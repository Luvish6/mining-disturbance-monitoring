No defensible candidate geometry was available for the negative leads. Retain natural riverbed, stable forest/agriculture/settlements, verified non-mining construction and potentially stable historical excavation as screening strata. Mynkree is UNKNOWN. Absence of reported activity, lease status or historical land-cover class cannot establish stability. The current accepted release contains zero polygons. There is insufficient independent evidence in every partition.

The [real-reference protocol](../reference/REAL_REFERENCE_PROTOCOL.md) requires actual dated independent observations for both windows, target-aligned conservative interiors inside the original district boundary, documented finite positional error at most 30 m, source provenance, independent second review and adequate positive/negative support in several buffered blocks per split. Neither application coordinates, raster resolution nor the Phase 3C relative alignment meets the positional evidence requirement.

2. **Lumshnong, validation lead 87:556.** Request from IBM Guwahati the dated as-built pit plans or orthoimagery underlying both 38MEG03016 inspections, coordinate reference, positional error and lease-revision history. Match the mine code and boundary before comparing actual pit outlines. Seek a replacement optical pair because the existing pair lacks site signal.
3. **Additional blocks and both evidence types.** Use the IBM inspection indexes and district archives to find dated mine-site observations in independent training blocks and additional validation/test blocks. Request matching seasonal imagery and official forestry, agriculture, settlement or construction records for negative strata across all splits. Assign blocks only after reliable georeferencing. Keep duplicate/shared observations in one evidence group.
4. **Resolve source geometry and corroborate.** Request the NRSC confirmations in the [provenance report](phase3d-provenance.md). For other leads, obtain authoritative CRS and accuracy before any spatial assignment. Field verification can confirm present-day identity and controls; historical records are needed to establish past surface state.
5. **Independent second review.** After both dates, geometry and rights are documented, obtain an independent review and adjudicate discrepancies under the existing admission protocol. Record rejected and ambiguous areas as UNKNOWN. No labels or model work were started.
