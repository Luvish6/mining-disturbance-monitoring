# Supervisor demonstration script

**0:00–0:45 · State the scope.** Open the dashboard and point to the validation banner. “This is a prototype for processing and inspecting satellite observations of surface change and river observation feasibility. The project has no admitted real mining labels or validated detection or river degradation results.”

**0:45–2:00 · Show the satellites.** Open Satellite Change Explorer. Switch between the before and after Sentinel-2 false-colour composites and an NDVI or NDTI difference, then show a Sentinel-1 VV or VH difference. “Radar measures microwave backscatter. Optical observations measure reflected light and need cloud and shadow screening. The project aligns the layers on a shared 30 m grid. Change layers show after-minus-before observations. Moisture, vegetation and terrain can affect the signal.” Magnify one layer and show observation counts. Explain that the tile contains real observations, with no classifier overlay.

**2:00–3:10 · Show where river observations survive.** Open River Monitoring. Switch between Sunapur and Leshka, choose a season, and filter for screened interior water. “The summary counts distinct dates. The detail table lists granules. Leshka was the only station with screened interior pixels in both seasons. That screen does not verify water quality.”

**3:10–4:00 · Show the failed comparison.** Open Leshka Feasibility. Point to five matched pairs, 12 common 10 m cells and the 25-cell pilot floor. “The fixed footprint was too small, so the dashboard withholds the seasonal spectral contrast. Rainfall comes from a coarse CHIRPS cell. Reservoir operations and discharge remain unresolved.”

**4:00–4:30 · Close on reproducibility.** Open Data Quality & Methodology. Show source collections and UTC dates. “The dashboard runs offline from saved products. The next scientific gate is independent reference evidence across the frozen partitions, followed by held-out ablation. A river impact assessment would also need stronger open-water support and hydrological and field evidence.”

If the server cannot start, walk through the four local browser screenshots in the same order. Keep the validation banner visible throughout.
