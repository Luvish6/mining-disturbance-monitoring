# Public mining benchmark feasibility

**GO for a bounded EuroMineNet 2023→2024 optical benchmark, with a pre-fit alignment and support gate.** Its target is a transition in the published annual EU mining-footprint masks. Indian supervised training and georeferenced inference remain under their existing evidence gates.

## Verified source and sample

- **Access and license.** The [RODARE v1 release](https://rodare.hzdr.de/record/4656), DOI 10.14278/rodare.4656, publishes the dataset under CC BY 4.0. The [record's direct ZIP](https://rodare.hzdr.de/record/4656/files/EuroMineNet.zip) returned exact HTTP 206 ranges and a total file size of 18,378,458,649 bytes. The alternate API storage URL returned HTTP 200 to a range probe, so use the verified record endpoint. The ZIP index contains 3,067 entries, including 2,663 TIFFs and four XML sidecars. No bulk download was made.
- **Label meaning.** The authors describe manually delineated mining-related cover using high-resolution reference interpretation, with checks against ancillary sources. The released loader binarizes the annual footprint masks, then uses their absolute difference. This is **expansion or contraction of the annotated footprint**, including infrastructure and reclamation effects. It does not identify separate activity events, detect changes that leave the footprint intact, or supply individually documented independent Indian seasonal labels. Per-pixel source dates and independent positional uncertainty were not established. [Author methodology](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14661v1#S3.SS2) · [Official change loader](https://github.com/AI4RS/EuroMineNet/blob/main/datasets/cd_dataset.py)
- **Imagery and geometry.** The 133 EU sites span 2015–2024. The paper describes annual Sentinel-2 L2A observations, with 10 and 20 m bands placed on a nominal 10 m grid. The Romania_1 TRAIN sample has co-gridded 296 × 320 EPSG:4326 10-band int16 TIFFs for 2023 and 2024, with B2/B3/B4/B5/B6/B7/B8/B8A/B11/B12; longitude spacing does not equal 10 m ground distance at this latitude. Exact sensing timestamps and NoData values were absent from both sampled TIFFs. Treat 2023 and 2024 as annual observation labels and leave exact dates unknown. The paired uint8 masks have 0/255 values and matching dimensions, with no CRS or geotransform. They support image-grid alignment checks and must remain ungeoreferenced unless matching is independently established. Four TRAIN mask pairs had 0, 283, 116 and 519 changed pixels respectively (Romania_1, Romania_8, Hungary_1, Austria_1), counting both directions. No validation or test pixels were sampled. The bounded archive reads totalled 4,259,372 bytes.
- **Splits.** The [publisher split file](https://github.com/AI4RS/EuroMineNet/blob/main/datasets/train_val_test_splits.py) has 102/14/17 entries and 101/14/17 unique sites. Germany_13 repeats within TRAIN, Greece_3 is unassigned, and the unique splits are disjoint. The archive contains all listed sites. Record the duplicate, deduplicate, and exclude the unassigned site. A distinct site ID does not prove spatial separation; check actual footprint intersections and neighboring mine complexes before fitting.
- **S2 compatibility.** Use the ten delivered optical channels. NDVI and NDTI are possible from the band definitions, but this pilot uses the delivered optical bands. SAR and terrain are absent. This experiment needs no GEE, and cannot use the frozen Indian 14-band loader or the RGB weights. The [Canada-wide benchmark](https://zenodo.org/records/18663545) is unnecessary for this pilot. Its archive documents 1,519 Google Earth RGB patches and mining-presence annotations, with screenshot dates distinct from satellite sensing dates. It supplies no documented paired Sentinel change target for this experiment.

## One prescribed experiment

**Acquisition.** Use the direct record ZIP above. Require a matching Content-Range 206, stable archive length, and CRC validation per member. Read the central directory and only `EuroMineNet/<site>/{image,label}/Year{2023,2024}.tif` for the sites below, plus any associated small metadata sidecars. Preserve source hashes and pin the publisher split file. The sites were chosen from complete two-year member listings before inspecting class support, by seed-42 country-prefix round-robin with SHA-256 ordering of prefixes and sites. Use these fixed lists.

| Split | Sites | Sum of compressed member bytes |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| TRAIN | Sweden_9, Czechia_3, Spain_15, Slovakia_2, Romania_8, Finland_4, Portuga_3, Poland_9, Hungary_1, Austria_1, Germany_14, Italy_1 | 187,217,870 |
| VALIDATION | Sweden_2, Czechia_2, Romania_4, Finland_7 | 116,956,397 |
| TEST | Sweden_1, Czechia_4, Slovakia_1, Romania_10 | 166,496,150 |

Eighty selected members total 470,670,417 compressed bytes and 522,438,316 unpacked bytes. Enforce **512 MiB total transfer**, including metadata, headers, sidecars and retries, and reserve **2 GiB disk** for local outputs. Acquire development first and open TEST bytes only after freezing both configurations. Fail closed if the server ignores ranges.

**Alignment and support gates.** Confirm native image-year grid agreement, band identities, known radiometry or consistent scaling, paired binary mask dimensions and stable visible alignment. Inspect mask-on-image overlays on development sites and apply the same eligibility checks to sealed test sites during final evaluation. Use the source pixel grid with no resampling or padding. Exclude unresolved grid, cloud/invalid-area and mask-alignment cases, and stop if a cross-split footprint overlap or shared mine complex cannot be resolved under the frozen roster. Check masks for actual transitions and report exclusions. Require both classes and positive support on at least two mines per split; withhold the planned model comparison if support fails. Do not replace sites based on foreground. Report any test failure as insufficient evidence.

**Models and budget.** Select up to 16 nonoverlapping 128 × 128 full windows per site, ordered by a seed-42 hash of site and row/column offset, before reading mask values. This caps TRAIN/VALIDATION/TEST at 192/64/64 pairs. Target is the XOR of binary annual masks on common valid support. RF baseline uses signed differences of all ten optical bands, 100 trees, depth 16, minimum leaf 2 and ≤100,000 distinct TRAIN pixels with per-site and class caps. Primary model is the existing width-16 Siamese U-Net instantiated with ten channels per date, TRAIN-only normalization, masked BCE, seed 42, Adam at .001, batch one, and at most five CPU epochs. Enforce a **30-minute combined CPU-only elapsed limit** and zero GEE calls. Select epoch by validation loss and both cutoffs from the prespecified grid .25, .35, .50, .65, .75, .90, .95 by maximum validation F1, breaking ties toward higher cutoffs. Freeze checkpoint, cutoff and scoring code before TEST. Score both on identical valid pixels once, with pooled/per-mine confusion matrices, precision, recall, F1, IoU and FPR, plus class support and undefined ratios as null. Four test sites and rare change limit generalization. No implementation, fitting or Indian evidence changes were made.
